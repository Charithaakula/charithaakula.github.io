Introduction

About me

At Meta Superintelligence Labs, I develop and train multimodal models for visual and spatial understanding, reasoning, and personalized, context-aware assistance. My work brings together visual representations, data development, post-training, and evaluation across text, images, and video to improve generalization and reliability.

I have identified overlooked capability gaps and built efficient, reusable methods to address them, often uncovering new possibilities in existing data and tools. I carry ideas from early experiments through model training, distillation, and deployment at scale.

Before Meta, I was on Millennium's four-person core AI team, reporting to the Head of AI, building enterprise RAG systems and agentic workflows and researching retrieval relevance. Earlier, I studied vision-centric multimodal models and diffusion models at NYU's CILVR Lab with Prof. Saining Xie. Before that, I spent six years as a quantitative researcher on Goldman Sachs' Interest Rates desk, developing trading, pricing, and risk models.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from NYU Courant and a B.Tech. in Computer Science (Honours) from IIT Bombay, with a minor in Electrical Engineering.

Scientific agents & evaluation

ReconcileBench

What happens when a persistent scientific agent has to revise its work after the evidence or analysis policy changes? I explored that question with Chanukya Vardhan Gujjula through ReconcileBench, a benchmark for keeping data, code, figures, reports, and decisions consistent. The work was selected for an oral presentation at the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Agentic AI for Biological Discovery (AgenticLS).
